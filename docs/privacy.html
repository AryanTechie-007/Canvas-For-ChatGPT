Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 28, 2026

Canvas for ChatGPT operates 100% client-side inside your browser. We respect your privacy and do not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

  • No ChatGPT prompts, conversations, or AI completions are ever read, logged, or transmitted.
  • No browsing history, cookies, authentication tokens, IP addresses, or telemetry are gathered.
  • Zero third-party analytics SDKs, trackers, or external backend servers are used.

2. Local Storage Only

All configuration preferences—including your selected backdrop URL, uploaded local media data URLs, saved backgrounds list, enabled toggle, opacity, blur, gradient, and sidebar frosting—are saved exclusively on your device via chrome.storage.local / browser.storage.local.

3. Permissions Justification

  • storage: Persists your visual preferences and saved wallpaper library locally.
  • unlimitedStorage: Supports large locally uploaded images, GIFs, and looping background videos without hitting standard 10 MB quotas.
  • Host permissions (https://chatgpt.com/*, https://chat.openai.com/*): Allows the content script to inject your chosen backdrop layer and transparent CSS styling exclusively on ChatGPT tabs.

Questions or concerns? Open an issue on GitHub Issues.
